1The Top 100 announcement (verbatim)

Milestone Update · 23 April 2026

The wait is over.

After an extensive evaluation of all the innovative prototypes submitted, we are excited to announce the Top 100 teams moving forward for prototype refinement phase in the Google Cloud Gen AI Academy APAC Edition.

Phase II: Selection Results — The Top 100 teams have been officially shortlisted. Shortlisted teams will receive $100 in Google Cloud credits. Refinement window is open until 30th April for final prototype submissions. Following the refinement phase, the Top 10 finalists will be selected to present at the Virtual Grand Finale on 11th May 2026.

— Team Hack2skill (on behalf of Google Cloud)

3Program facts & selection funnel

FieldDetail
ProgramGoogle Cloud Gen AI Academy, Asia-Pacific Edition, Cohort 1, 2026
OperatorHack2skill, "on behalf of Google Cloud"
RegionAsia-Pacific (India, Singapore, Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia, Philippines, Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Nepal, South Korea, Hong Kong, Taiwan, Australia, New Zealand and others)
Format"Learn, Challenge, Build". Multi-track workshops, codelabs, an MCQ assessment, and a prototype submission, culminating in a hackathon with a virtual finale.
Prize poolUp to US$10,000 per cohort
Top 100 selectionAnnounced 23 April 2026, after evaluation of all prototype submissions. Each Top 100 team received US$100 in Google Cloud credits.
Top 10 selectionAnnounced 7 May 2026. Finalists selected from the Top 100 after the prototype refinement phase.
Virtual Grand Finale11 May 2026. Top 10 teams presented to a panel of Google Cloud experts.
Winners (12 May 2026)1st: Jangan Tanya Kapan Nikah · 2nd: When I'm With You · 3rd: Dheeraj Kumar Jayprakash Yadav. Several teams additionally received "Performance Excellence Awards".
Gen AI Elite ClubAn "invite-only alumni network". Hack2skill stated 50 seats per cohort (25 students and 25 professionals), with access to Google Developer community programs, GDP newsletter recognition, and program feature stories.
Issuer's own framing"Selected from thousands of submissions across APAC by a panel of Google Cloud experts" (Hack2skill, 12 May 2026 winners email).
